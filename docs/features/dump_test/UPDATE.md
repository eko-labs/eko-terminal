# Dump test

Every coin now carries one line next to its verdict: what EKO estimates would happen to the price if the coin's insiders sold everything they hold right now, and what $100 held today would then be worth, as in "If insiders dump: −83% · your $100 → $17". The coin page breaks it down by group (the dev, wallets the dev funded, bundles, snipers and early buyers still holding), each shown as a bar coloured by how far it would drop the price. The coin lists gain a Dump column you can sort by, and if you watch or hold a coin you get an alert when its insiders come to hold enough to drop it much further.
