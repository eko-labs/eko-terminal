# Simple view

EKO now opens in a Simple view, with a big Simple | Full switch at the top of every page that brings back the full terminal in one tap. A coin's page in Simple reads the way traders expect: market cap, price, liquidity, volume, supply, bonding curve, tax and the 5m, 1h and 24h moves in one strip, the live chart with its timeframes, buys and sells for 5 minutes, an hour or a day, the guarded trade panel, holder stats (top 10, dev, bundlers, fresh wallets, insiders, snipers) and a table of the latest trades with each trade's market cap and the dev's trades marked. Right under the strip, EKO answers first: Looks OK, Be careful, Don't buy or Still checking, a one-word steer, up to three plain reasons, and whether you can sell it, what $100 gets back and how much the dev, the top ten and the bundles hold; a coin EKO says not to buy offers a safer coin before its trade panel. The coins list shows what is busy right now and every coin's answer at a glance, with the same live heat as the full Radar, quick filters in trench words (Fresh, Hot, Pumping, Buyers winning, About to bond, Bonded), each explained in one line, and sorts by hottest, newest, biggest movers, most traded or market cap; and a short walkthrough lights up one thing at a time on your first visit (Help can replay it). Your choice is kept in this browser, and Full is the terminal exactly as before.
