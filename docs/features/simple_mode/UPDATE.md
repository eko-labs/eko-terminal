# Simple view

EKO now opens in a Simple view made for anyone who has never traded, and a big Simple | Full switch at the top of every page brings back the full terminal in one tap. In Simple, every coin answers one question in a word or two (Looks OK, Be careful, Don't buy or Still checking) with up to three plain reasons, then plain facts with the terminal's own gauges: can you sell it, what $100 gets back, how it moved today, how much money is behind it, how much the top ten own and how busy it is, plus the live chart, who is buying and one big button to buy it through your own wallet, or to find a safer coin. The coins list shows what is busy right now and every coin's answer at a glance, with the same live heat as the full Radar, quick filters in trench words (Fresh, Hot, Pumping, Buyers winning, About to bond, Bonded), each explained in one line, and sorts by hottest, newest, biggest movers, most traded or market cap; and a short walkthrough lights up one thing at a time on your first visit (Help can replay it). Your choice is kept in this browser, and Full is the terminal exactly as before.
